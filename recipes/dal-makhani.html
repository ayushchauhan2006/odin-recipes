Dal Makhani

Description

Dal makhani is a rich, creamy, and buttery North Indian lentil dish made with whole black lentils and kidney beans.

Ingredients

  • Black Lentis
  • Kidney Beans
  • Onion and Tomatoes
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Whole Spices
  • Butter and Ghee
  • Heavy Cream
  • Chilli and Garam Masala
  • Kasuri Methi

Steps

  1. Soak the black lentils and kidney beans overnight for 8 to 9 hours.
  2. Drain the soaking water and rinse the lentils thoroughly in fresh water.
  3. Boil the lentils in a pressure cooker with fresh water and salt until completely mashable
  4. Mash a few cooked lentils with a spoon to make the final texture naturally creamy.
  5. Melt butter and ghee in a deep pan and fry the whole spices for a minute.
  6. Sauté the finely chopped onions in the hot fat until they turn golden brown.
  7. Cook the ginger-garlic paste with the onions until the raw smell goes away.
  8. Simmer the tomato puree with red chili powder and turmeric until oil separates.
  9. Mix the boiled lentils and their cooking liquid into the fried tomato masala base.
  10. Simmer the mixture on low heat for 30 minutes while stirring frequently.
  11. Season the dal with garam masala and crushed kasuri methi near the end.
  12. Finish by stirring in heavy cream and topping with an extra dollop of butter.
