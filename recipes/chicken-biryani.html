Chicken Biryani

Home

Description

Chicken biryani is a famous South Asian mixed rice dish made with basmati rice, spiced chicken, and aromatic herbs. It features tender, marinated meat, fluffy long-grain rice, and rich spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Ages Basmati Rice
  • Yogurt and Lemon
  • Onions,Ginger and Garlic
  • Ghee or Oil
  • Fresh Mint and Coriander
  • Whole Spices
  • Saffron Milk

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry sliced onions in ghee or oil until golden brown and crispy.
  3. Boil water with whole spices and salt.
  4. Cook washed basmati rice in the boiling water until 70% done.
  5. Drain the rice and set it aside.
  6. Sear the marinated chicken in the onion oil until cooked through.
  7. Layer the cooked chicken gravy and parboiled rice in a heavy pot.
  8. Top the layers with fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk.
  9. Seal the pot tightly with a lid to trap the steam.
  10. Cook on low heat for 15 minutes to infuse flavours.
