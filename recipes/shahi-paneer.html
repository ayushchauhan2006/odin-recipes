Shahi Paneer

Home

Description

Shahi paneer is a rich, royal North Indian dish made with soft paneer cubes in a thick, creamy gravy of yogurt, cream, and ground nuts.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Onions,Tomatoes and Ginger-Garlic
  • Cashews and Almonds
  • Ghee or Butter
  • Fresh Cream or malai
  • Whole Spices
  • Ground Spices
  • Saffron or Sugar

Steps

  1. Boil onions, tomatoes, cashews, ginger, and garlic in water until soft.
  2. Blend the cooled mixture into a smooth, fine puree.
  3. Sauté whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon) in ghee or butter.
  4. Pour the blended puree into the pot with the spices.
  5. Add Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, and salt to the gravy.
  6. Simmer the sauce on low heat until the oil separates.
  7. Stir in a pinch of sugar, garam masala, and fresh cream.
  8. Add the paneer cubes gently into the simmering gravy.
  9. Cook for 5 minutes on low heat so paneer absorbs flavors.
  10. Garnish with crushed kasuri methi and a splash of extra cream.
